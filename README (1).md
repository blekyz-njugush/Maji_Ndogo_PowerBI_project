# Maji Ndogo Water Infrastructure and Budget Allocation Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze water infrastructure improvements, budget allocation, and service delivery across the Maji Ndogo national water system.

The dashboard provides national and provincial-level insights into water access initiatives across five provinces: **Akatsi, Amanzi, Hawassa, Kilimani, and Sokoto**. It enables stakeholders to explore infrastructure investments, evaluate improvement efforts, and identify potential disparities in water service delivery between rural and urban communities.

By combining key performance indicators (KPIs), budget analysis, and interactive visualizations, the project supports data-driven decision-making on water infrastructure development and resource allocation.

## Key Insights and Features

### 1. Regional Performance Analysis
- Compare population figures, infrastructure improvements, and budget allocation across the five provinces.
- Identify regional differences in investment and infrastructure development.
- Explore provincial performance through interactive dashboard elements.

### 2. Budget Allocation and Spending Analysis
- Examine the distribution of the **$147 million national budget** and the **$40 million allocated to Sokoto**.
- Analyze investment across infrastructure types, including drilled wells, reverse osmosis (RO) filters, and public taps.
- Compare spending patterns to support more informed resource allocation.

### 3. Infrastructure Improvement Effectiveness
- Track completed infrastructure improvements across different intervention types.
- Compare the volume and associated costs of improvement initiatives.
- Support the assessment of investment priorities and infrastructure development outcomes.

### 4. Rural–Urban Disparity Analysis
- Compare population distribution and infrastructure spending between rural and urban communities.
- Highlight potential inequalities in resource allocation.
- Provide insights that can help prioritize underserved areas.

### 5. Interactive Dashboard Navigation
- Implement a **Province/Improvement toggle** to dynamically switch the focus of the main visualization.
- Use Power BI bookmarks to control visual states and improve report navigation.
- Enable users to explore national and provincial insights through an intuitive interface.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation, where applicable.
- **Data Modeling** — Organizing data and relationships to support analysis.
- **Power BI Bookmarks** — Implementing interactive visual toggles and navigation.
- **Interactive Visualizations and KPIs** — Presenting key metrics and supporting exploratory analysis.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate water infrastructure investment across provinces.
- Analyze budget allocation across different infrastructure interventions.
- Compare rural and urban spending patterns.
- Monitor infrastructure improvement activities.
- Provide stakeholders with actionable insights into water access challenges and investment priorities.

## Key Takeaway

The Maji Ndogo Water Infrastructure and Budget Allocation Analysis demonstrates how **Power BI can transform infrastructure and budget data into interactive, decision-support dashboards**. By combining regional comparisons, investment analysis, and rural–urban disparity assessments, the project provides a foundation for identifying service gaps and informing future water infrastructure planning.
